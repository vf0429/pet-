## 2. Problem Statement

| Problem Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Veterinary Resource Shortage | Hong Kong's veterinary resources are limited, and pet owners often face long waiting times, uneven service quality, and difficulty accessing timely care guidance. |
| Fragmented Medical Records | Pet medical records are usually stored separately across clinics, making it hard for owners and providers to access a complete health history. |
| Low Transparency and Trust | Pet owners often rely on scattered online information when choosing clinics or services, with limited trustworthy review and quality signals. |
| Weak Post-Visit Engagement | After consultations, there is often no structured follow-up, health tracking, or continuity of care, resulting in lower user retention and weaker long-term health outcomes. |
| Disconnected Ecosystem | Clinics, merchants, service providers, and insurers operate independently, limiting referrals, collaboration, and cross-service conversion opportunities. |

## 3. Solution

| Solution Module | Description |
|---|---|
| AI Triage & Guidance | Petwell provides AI-assisted triage and symptom guidance to help pet owners identify urgency, improve care access, and reduce unnecessary friction before clinic visits. |
| Shared Health Records | Petwell builds a structured digital pet health record that can be continuously updated and referenced across service touchpoints, improving continuity of care. |
| Trust & Review Mechanism | Petwell introduces a more transparent and trustworthy review layer, helping users make better decisions while rewarding high-quality providers. |
| Clinic SaaS Tools | Petwell offers clinics digital tools for patient records, follow-up, CRM, and operational workflows to improve efficiency and retention. |
| Ecosystem Platform | Petwell connects clinics, merchants, services, and insurance partners into a single ecosystem to unlock referrals, repeat purchase, and ecosystem-level collaboration. |
